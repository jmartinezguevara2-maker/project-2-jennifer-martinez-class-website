Lets go back in time!

Betty Boop first appeared in 1930 in an animated short produced by Fleischer Studios. The character became known for her distinctive design, expressive animation, music, and playful personality.

Fleischer Studios was founded by brothers Max and Dave Fleischer. Its cartoons developed a style that felt different from many other animated films of the era, mixing surreal humor, jazz music, and lively city settings.

From the early cartoons

Betty's first appearances reflected the experimental energy of early sound cartoons. Her look and stories changed over time as animation and popular culture evolved.
